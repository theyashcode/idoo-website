Yash Chaudhary

IDOO started in 2019 as a one-person freelance setup working out of a small room in New Delhi, taking on any website job that paid the bills. Today the studio is a full team working with retailers, fintech startups and family businesses across India, but the habit of answering every client call personally has never changed.

Every project still goes through the same three checks before it ships: does it load fast on a basic phone, does it make sense to someone who has never used the product before, and would the team be proud to put their name on it.

  • How fast can you deliver?

    Most brochure websites are ready in 2–3 weeks. Larger builds with custom dashboards or apps usually take 6–10 weeks, and we agree a timeline before any work starts.

  • Do I own the code?

    Yes. Once the project is paid for in full, the complete source code and design files are handed over to you with no lock-in.

  • Do you work outside Delhi?

    We work with clients across India over video calls and email, and can meet in person for teams based in Delhi NCR.

  • How do we get started?

    Call or WhatsApp us, tell us what you're building, and we'll set up a free 20-minute discovery call within a day.

  • Do you offer support after launch?

    Every project includes 30 days of free support after go-live, and ongoing maintenance plans are available after that.
